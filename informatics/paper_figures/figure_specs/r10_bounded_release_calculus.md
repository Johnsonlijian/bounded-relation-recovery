# R10 figure design brief — bounded-release calculus

## Figure 12: action-preserving semantics and controls

- **Argument role:** primary mechanism figure. It makes the paper's central unit visible: a typed contract is reduced by fixed predicates to one computational permission; operational use remains outside the reducer.
- **Takeaway:** the reducer emits `REJECT_NONEXECUTABLE`, `RETAIN_BOUNDED_CLAIM`, or `ALLOW_BOUNDED_QUERY`; a stronger or open gate can only block/demote release.
- **Data/evidence:** `src/brr/schema.py`, `src/brr/actions.py`, `outputs/r10_predicate_action_matrix.csv`, and `outputs/r10_cross_table_modulus_transfer.csv`.
- **Evidence boundary:** panel B is cross-table transfer within the same Zhang source and is explicitly not independent physical validation. Panel A is a deterministic method control.
- **Construction:** Python/Matplotlib generated by `src/r10_super_uplift_evidence.py`; no AI-generated visual content and no unverified data.
- **Export contract:** PDF and SVG masters plus 220 dpi PNG preview. Text remains vector in PDF/SVG; source script and CSVs are included in the reproducibility slice.

## Figure 13: scale non-identification

- **Argument role:** negative result supporting the distinction between shape identification and amplitude identification.
- **Takeaway:** each geometry group has a non-empty positive scale interval, while the intersection of all four groups is empty at the displayed half-unit; a global scale is therefore not a valid hidden assumption.
- **Data/evidence:** `data/source_table_transcription.csv`, `outputs/r10_global_scale_check.json`.
- **Evidence boundary:** this is an interval consequence for the declared Zhang table contract, not an estimate of physical modulus uncertainty and not an out-of-sample validation.
- **Construction/export:** same deterministic Python/Matplotlib script and PDF/SVG/PNG contract as Figure 12.
